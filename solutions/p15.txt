section: 유형 09 극한값의 계산 (4)
@ 29 | ②\ 2
\sqrt{x^2+4x}-x=\frac{4x}{\sqrt{x^2+4x}+x}=\frac{4}{\sqrt{1+\frac4x}+1}\to\frac42=2
@ 30 | ③\ 6
\sqrt{3x^2+ax}-\sqrt{3x^2}=\frac{ax}{\sqrt{3x^2+ax}+\sqrt3x}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{3+\frac ax}+\sqrt3}\to\frac{a}{2\sqrt3}
\frac{a}{2\sqrt3}=\sqrt3\ \Rightarrow\ a=6
@ 31 | ④\ -\frac92
x=-t\ (t\to\infty):\ \sqrt{t^2-9t}-t=\frac{-9t}{\sqrt{t^2-9t}+t}=\frac{-9}{\sqrt{1-\frac9t}+1}\to-\frac92
@ 32 | ④\ 2
\sqrt{x^2+2x}-\sqrt{x^2+x}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+2x}+\sqrt{x^2+x}}\to\frac12
\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+2x}-\sqrt{x^2+x}}=\frac{1}{\frac12}=2
@ 33 | ③\ 3
\sqrt{x^2+ax}-\sqrt{x^2-ax}=\frac{2ax}{\sqrt{x^2+ax}+\sqrt{x^2-ax}}\to\frac{2a}{2}=a
\frac1a=\frac13\ \Rightarrow\ a=3
@ 34 | ②\ \frac{2\sqrt2}{3}
\text{분자}=\frac{4x}{\sqrt{x^2+2x}+\sqrt{x^2-2x}}\to\frac42=2
\text{분모}=\frac{6x}{\sqrt{2x^2+3x}+\sqrt{2x^2-3x}}\to\frac{6}{2\sqrt2}=\frac{3}{\sqrt2}
2\div\frac3{\sqrt2}=\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}
