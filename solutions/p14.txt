@ 23 | ④\ \frac18
\frac{\sqrt{x+2}-2}{x(x-2)}=\frac{(x+2)-4}{x(x-2)(\sqrt{x+2}+2)}=\frac{1}{x(\sqrt{x+2}+2)}
\lim_{x\to2}\frac{1}{x(\sqrt{x+2}+2)}=\frac1{2\cdot4}=\frac18
@ 24 | ③\ -6
\frac{(x-2)(x+1)}{\sqrt{x+2}-1}=\frac{(x-2)(x+1)(\sqrt{x+2}+1)}{(x+2)-1}=(x-2)(\sqrt{x+2}+1)
\lim_{x\to-1}(x-2)(\sqrt{x+2}+1)=(-3)\cdot2=-6
