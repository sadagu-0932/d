section: 유형 14 함수의 극한의 활용
@ 53 | ④\ 2\sqrt2
x^2=-x+t\Rightarrow x^2+x-t=0,\ \text{두 근 }\alpha<\beta
\beta-\alpha=\sqrt{1+4t},\quad \overline{AB}=\sqrt2(\beta-\alpha)=\sqrt2\sqrt{1+4t}
f(t)-\sqrt2=\sqrt2(\sqrt{1+4t}-1)=\frac{4\sqrt2\,t}{\sqrt{1+4t}+1}
\lim_{t\to0+}\frac{f(t)-\sqrt2}{t}=\lim_{t\to0+}\frac{4\sqrt2}{\sqrt{1+4t}+1}=2\sqrt2
@ 54 | \frac12
A(m,\ m^2+m),\quad B(m,\ m^2)\Rightarrow\overline{AB}=m
S(m)=\frac12\cdot m\cdot m=\frac{m^2}{2}
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{m^2}{2(m^2+1)}=\frac12
@ 55 | ①\ \frac{\sqrt2}{2}
A\text{ 의 }y\text{좌표 }k:\ x=\frac{k^2-1}{2},\ k=-\frac{k^2-1}{2}+t\Rightarrow k^2+2k-(2t+1)=0
k=-1+\sqrt{2t+2},\quad B(t,0)
S(t)=\frac12\cdot t\cdot(\sqrt{2t+2}-1)
\lim_{t\to\infty}\frac{S(t)}{t\sqrt t}=\lim_{t\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt{2t+2}-1}{2\sqrt t}=\lim_{t\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt{2+\frac2t}-\frac1{\sqrt t}}{2}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}
@ 56 | ③\ \frac14
A(a,\sqrt a):\ a^2+a=m^2\Rightarrow a=\frac{-1+\sqrt{1+4m^2}}{2}=\frac{2m^2}{\sqrt{1+4m^2}+1}
S(m)=\frac12\cdot\overline{HA}\cdot\overline{OH}=\frac12\cdot a\cdot\sqrt a\Rightarrow\{S(m)\}^2=\frac{a^3}{4}
\frac{a}{m^2}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{1+4m^2}+1}\to1\quad(m\to0+)
\lim_{m\to0+}\frac{\{S(m)\}^2}{m^6}=\frac14\cdot1^3=\frac14
